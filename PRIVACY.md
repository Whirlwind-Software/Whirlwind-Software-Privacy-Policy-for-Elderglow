# Privacy Policy for Elderglow

**Last updated: 20 October 2026**

Elderglow is a tower defence game for Android made by **Whirlwind Software**. This policy
describes what it does with your information.

## The short version

**Elderglow has no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting and no server of
its own.** Your progress and settings stay on your phone. The game shows ads from
**Google AdMob**, and that is the one place where information leaves your device.

## What Elderglow stores, and where

All of it is in the game's own private storage on your device, which other apps
cannot read. None of it is sent to us.

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Your progress: walks won, stars, the Elder's tree, species met | The app's private storage |
| Settings: sound, music, vibration | The app's private storage |
| A walk left half-way, to go on with later | The app's private storage |
| How many walks since the last full-screen ad, and whether ads were paid away | The app's private storage |

Uninstalling the game deletes all of the above.

## Ads

Elderglow shows ads served by **Google AdMob**:

- a **rewarded ad**, only when you choose to watch one ("Rise again" after a loss);
- an occasional **full-screen ad between walks**, never during one.

To show ads and count them, Google may collect your device's **advertising ID**, your
approximate location (from your IP address), and information about your device and how
you interact with ads. Google uses this as described in
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive
this information ourselves, beyond totals of ads shown and earned.

## Your choices

If you are in the **EEA, the UK or Switzerland**, the game asks for your consent before
ads are personalised. If you are in a **US state with its own privacy law**, you can opt
out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. Either way you can change your
choice at any time in the game: **Settings → Privacy choices**.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings
(**Settings → Google → Ads**), and see or change how Google uses your data in
[My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).

## Purchases

If you buy something in the game (such as removing the full-screen ads), the payment is
handled entirely by **Google Play** under the Google Privacy Policy. We receive only the
confirmation that the purchase was made, never your payment details.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have the
right to see, correct or delete personal data about you, and to object to its use. We
keep no personal data about you on our side, so there is nothing for us to hand over or
delete: what the game keeps is on your phone and goes when you uninstall it. For the data
Google collects for ads, use the choices above. You can always write to us with a request.

## Children

Elderglow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from them.

## Changes to this policy

If the game starts to use information in a new way, this page is updated first, and the
date at the top changes.
